How we work

Every Source Accessibility™ project is tailored. We start by understanding your product and context, then design a testing and research programme that reflects differently disabled experiences and the realities of your teams.

  1. 1

    Understand your product & goals

    We map your key journeys, constraints and timelines so testing reflects how people actually use your service – whether you’re new to accessibility or deepening existing work.

  2. 2

    Assistive tech health check & lived experience

    We conduct an assistive technology ‘health-check’ to inform next steps, then test with our disabled panel for genuine lived experience sessions your team can attend or replay.

  3. 3

    Turn findings into action

    You get clear, prioritised recommendations, recordings you can share internally, and ongoing support – including future-ready agent testing – so teams know what to fix now and what to plan for next.

Important to you, important to us

Source Accessibility™ regularly works with sensitive information, including disability-related details that are treated as special category data under UK GDPR.

  • Data protection & GDPR: We minimise data, store it securely and only use it for agreed research and testing purposes.
  • Security: Ways of working are aligned with Cyber Essentials good practice to protect client and participant data.
  • Professional standards: Led by an IAAP member with appropriate professional indemnity and public liability cover for consultancy and research work.
  • Sustainability: Remote-first by default, reducing travel while still enabling meaningful participation from diverse disabled users.

Full details are available in our Accessibility statement and Privacy & data pages.
